What We Do

Who we are

Tech Shujaaz is a passionate team dedicated to enhancing mental wellness among Kenyan youth through innovative digital solutions and community engagement. Our mission is to create a supportive and resourceful platform that empowers, educates, and connects young people on their mental wellness journeys.

#WeGetIt.

What we Do

Vijana Mental Hub works with mental health partners, professionals, service providers, and event organizers to help young people stay informed about the mental health resources available to them.

Our goal is to make it easier for young people to check in, discover, and access support in one place. This may include therapy and counselling, support groups, wellness activities, professional meetups, awareness events, community gatherings, and other mental health resources.
